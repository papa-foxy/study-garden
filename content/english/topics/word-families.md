vocabulary:
  - word: decide
    meaning: To choose after thinking.
    example: We will decide after lunch.
  - word: decision
    meaning: A choice that someone makes.
    example: It was a difficult decision.
  - word: decisive
    meaning: Able to make choices clearly and quickly.
    example: The team needs a decisive leader.

Words from one family share a root idea. **Decide** is a verb, **decision** is a noun, and **decisive** is an adjective. Learning the words in sentences shows how each one fits.
